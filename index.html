What we do

Commercial cleaning and building maintenance services for DC-area properties

Commercial cleaning keeps your building presentable day to day. Building maintenance protects it from water, wear, and birds. Diamond handles both, so you’re not coordinating separate vendors for the inside and the outside.

5 services

Commercial Cleaning All cleaning

  • Janitorial ServicesRoutine cleaning of offices, restrooms, and common areas on a set schedule.
  • Green CleaningCleaning with lower-toxicity products and methods.
  • Window CleaningInterior and exterior glass, from storefronts to multi-story facades.
  • Power WashingHigh-pressure cleaning for concrete, sidewalks, garages, and other hard surfaces.
  • Soft WashingLow-pressure washing for siding, roofs, and surfaces pressure would damage.

Maintenance Services All maintenance
